### The Washington Post. Mar. 17, 2026
I think this is a very interesting data story. 

It uses big data analysis to examine the levels of exposure and adaptability across different jobs in order to assess how likely AI is to replace these jobs in the future. It also provides a highly effective interactive scatter plot, where the size, location or color of the points may represent factors such as employment numbers, industry categories, or gender ratios, helping readers more easily understand trends within complex data.

However, I believe there is also a problem: we do not know exactly what “exposure” and “adaptability” exactly refer to, nor how they are being quantified or measured.
