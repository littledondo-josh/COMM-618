# Data Story 8
## [4 charts show where money is going in the midterms — and who has the most cash](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/23/nx-s1-5795841/democrats-senate-fundraising-republican-maga-cash-on-hand-campaign-finance)
### NPR, April 10, 2026

I think NPR’s story on midterm election fundraising is very interesting and is a strong example of a data story.

The strength of this story lies in how it uses campaign finance data to explain the early political momentum of the 2026 midterm elections. Through four charts, the story compares Democratic candidates’ fundraising advantages with the cash reserves held by Republican national party organizations and super PACs. It also highlights the pressure created by primary challenges and generational turnover within the parties.

At the same time, the visualizations are very effective. The four charts use different formats, including a line chart, two different types of bar charts, and a scatterplot. These charts clearly present the distribution, comparisons, and changes in midterm campaign funding.

However, I think the interpretation of the data could be stronger in some respects. For example, the story does not further explain how fundraising levels are related to candidates’ likelihood of electoral success, which would have helped readers better understand the significance of the data.
