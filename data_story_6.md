# Data Story 6
## [Who Knew ‘Slick’ Gavin Newsom Was Such an Economic Maestro?](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-04-06/california-s-economic-boom-proves-gavin-newsom-s-critics-wrong)
### Bloomberg April. 07, 2026

I think this is an interesting data story that shows the contrast between long-standing media narratives about California’s economy under Gavin Newsom and the actual economic data.

Although public opinion has often emphasized various problems in California’s economy, the data still suggests strong economic growth. During Newsom’s tenure, California’s economic growth rate has not only exceeded all developed economies, including the United States, but also outperformed emerging economies such as China. In addition, the story uses many other data points to show a very positive picture of California’s economy, such as the significant increase in corporate M&A activity during Newsom’s time in office, as well as much higher returns from investing in California-based companies compared to others.

The story includes a large amount of data, but most of it is presented in text form rather than visualized with charts, so I think it is not very intuitive.
