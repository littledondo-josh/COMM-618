# Data Story 2026-03-24
## [Which Countries Depend the Most on Persian Gulf Oil and Gas](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2026/03/23/business/energy-environment/oil-importers-middle-east-iran-war.html)
### The New York Times. 24, March

I think this is a straightforward and clear data story that presents the share of energy imports from Gulf countries to help readers understand which continents or countries are most and least vulnerable in terms of energy during the Iran war, which has led to a blockade of the Persian Gulf, a region that accounts for about one-fifth of the world’s energy supply.

The story does an excellent job of presenting data through interactive charts, allowing readers to see that Asian and African countries are more severely impacted by the Iran war, while European and American countries appear more resilient.

The methodology section at the end of the story is also essential, as it clearly explains where the data comes from, how the data was compiled, and what is included in the definition of energy.
