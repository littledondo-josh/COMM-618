# Data Story 9
## [The Year of the Molotov Cocktail: American Antigovernment Violence Hits a 30-Year High](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/the-year-of-the-molotov-cocktail-american-antigovernment-violence-hits-a-30-year-high-bca03a67)
### The Wall Street Journal April. 27, 2026

This data story uses the recent assassination incident of the White House Correspondents’ Dinner as a news hook and presents data showing trends in political violence in the United States over the past three decades, including numbers, the ideological distribution of incidents and the types of weapons used.

I think the topic is very compelling because it addresses one of the questions people are most eager to understand right now. However, I also believe the story has some limitations. It does not place the data within a broader historical or political context, so the piece reads more like a data analysis  than a fully developed piece of data-driven story.

At the same time, I think the second chart may be somewhat misleading. Because the scale of the axis intervals is too large, small differences in the data appear visually exaggerated in the chart, which could give readers an inaccurate impression of the magnitude of those differences.
